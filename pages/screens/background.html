Background

Now we get to the best part, the floating shape background. Here is how it works

3 Steps

1. Appear

Goes to the center of the screen and appears

2. Determine

Calculates the line and rotation and adjusts OrgX/OrgY

3. Move

Same movement script, but after each movement, finds new OrgX/OrgY
