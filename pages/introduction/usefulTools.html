3. Smooth Movement Scripts

This is the most important script, and it bassically handles all of the smooth movement you see. Knowing how it works is crucial, and there is an example below

To adjust where you want a sprite, please adjust Org X/Org Y.

To adjust how fast the sprite moves, adjust OFFSET_SPEED.

How it works is it figures out the distance to the desired position from your current x position and divides it by offset speed. Then it moves that same amount and repeats the process, creating smooth movement!
